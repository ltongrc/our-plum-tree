A little story from our yard

Our Plum Tree

01   THE TREE

A yearly treat

This is the plum tree in our yard. Every year, it gives us delicious plums that our family looks forward to enjoying. The plums turn yellowish when they are ripe, and their color seems to help them go unnoticed by birds and bugs, leaving more fruit for us to enjoy.

02   THE LITTLE BIRD

A familiar little friend

If you look closely, you can spot a little bird ornament hanging from one of the branches. We put it there in 2022, and it has been keeping our tree company for three or four years!
